Checks before you offer

  • EPC certificate: rating, floor area, heating, wall and roof type. Rented homes are due to need EPC C by October 2030.
  • Homes to rent in {{ g.outcode }} and homes for sale in {{ g.outcode }}: the rent you can expect and the competition.
  • Landlord licensing in {{ g.district }}: some streets need a council licence before letting. Add the fee under "Costs and search settings".
  • Title register, £7: owner, price paid, and whether a lender has a charge.
  • A RICS Level 2 survey, and for a refurbished house the electrical and gas certificates.
  • Make the offer by phone, then confirm by email "subject to contract and survey" with proof of funds or a mortgage agreement in principle.
